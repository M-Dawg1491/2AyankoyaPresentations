Razor-Knuckles

Could this karmic hummiliation go any worse for Douglas? Back when he was a mercenary for hire, he was basically on top of the world! But it ended poorly when he lost his arms to a mob boss and left to die in an inceneratior and pushed off a cliff, along with having to spend prison time with some abelist immates.
And when he eventually gets released some years later, filled with vengance in his heart (and newfound love from someone special), he dons a set of razor-sharp metal blades where his hands used to be and adventures as Razor-Knuckles!
On a one man crusade to get money for new hands (made of flesh and bone) and to get vengance on the mob boss and to an extent, the world. But how far is he willing to push himself (and the people around him) to get what he wants? And will he ever find peace in his life again?
