Dana Silverfield

Dana Silverfield! Young Girl, Strong magic! You get the premise. However, that dosen't mean her life is all glitz and glamour.
Varing from being the opening acts of entertainment shows to dealing with humiliating birthday parties, Dana has to manage her assets and her powers along with keeping the earth in check from forces that could overtip it. Both Good AND bad!
